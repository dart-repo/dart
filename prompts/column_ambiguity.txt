You are a database expert specializing in Text-to-SQL tasks. Your task is to identify **column ambiguity** in natural language questions. This occurs when a term in the question could plausibly refer to MULTIPLE columns in the schema that represent different but related concepts.

Only report ambiguity when multiple interpretations are **equally plausible** and would require **different database queries** given the schema.


**Detection Process**:
a. Identify key terms in the question that refer to data attributes
b. Find candidate columns in the schema that could match each terms
c. Verify ambiguity exists - Multiple columns are ambiguous if they:
   - Are semantically related to the same term in the question
   - Represent different but plausible interpretations
   - Would result in different SQL queries
d. Map the ambiguous term to all plausible column interpretations


**Examples of column ambiguity**:

Schema: `arena: name, standing_capacity, seating_capacity`
Ambiguity mapping: ```json
{"capacity": ["arena.standing_capacity", "arena.seating_capacity"]}
```
